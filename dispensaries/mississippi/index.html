01Do I need a medical card?

It depends on the state. Mississippi and Oklahoma run medical cannabis programs, so buying there requires a valid medical card. Maryland and New York allow adult-use purchases for anyone 21 or older with a valid photo ID, and both also run medical programs.

General information, not medical or legal advice. Rules differ by state and can change; check your store's page before you visit.
