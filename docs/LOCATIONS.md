## Gaps

- **Hours not captured (6 stores):** Greenwood, Natchez, Ocean Springs, Pearl, Tupelo (Saltillo) and Buffalo. The Mississippi hub card links straight to the shop ("VIEW HOURS & SHOP"), and Buffalo's only captured sources (the Order Online card and the opening post) give no hours. `hours` is `[]` for these.
- **About copy not captured (5 stores):** Greenwood, Natchez, Ocean Springs, Pearl, Tupelo. No main-site store page exists for them; `about` is `[]`.
- **No photo (6 stores):** all six Mississippi stores. No captured page shows a photo of them; `photo` is `null`.
- **State blurbs:** Mississippi and Oklahoma hubs carry store cards only, so their `blurb` is empty.
- **Deals:** no Mississippi deals page was captured; the Mississippi daily deals come from the Oxford store page and may not apply to every Mississippi store. No New York deals were captured.
- **Rewards:** no New York rewards page was captured (only the join link on rewards.json).
- **Lawton address:** the store page and Order Online card say "2629 NW Cache Rd"; the Oklahoma hub card says "2629 NW Cache Rd - Suite 3". The store page version is used.
- **Baltimore address:** the store page heading line reads "5975 S BELAIR RD"; its body copy and the Order Online card say "5975 Belair Rd". "5975 Belair Rd" is used.
- **Natchez phone:** the Order Online card lists (662) 638-3008, which is the Oxford number; the Mississippi hub card's (601) 653-0400 is used.
- **Chickasha hours:** the page lists 8:00AM-10:00PM daily, but its own "open late" answer says 8:00 am to 12:00 am; the hours list is used and that answer is left out.
- **Store names:** Oklahoma, Oxford and Baltimore keep the long names from their store pages (`name`); every store also has `shortName` = "Star Buds <City>". Tupelo's address is in Saltillo (`locality`).
- **Captured copy is old:** Oklahoma pages say "7 Oklahoma locations" while the main site links 4 Oklahoma stores on the shop; those sentences are not used. None of the captured facts could be checked against the live shop.
