Know before you go

01This is my first time visiting Star Buds. What should I bring with me?

All visitors must be at least 21 years of age and must provide a valid photo ID. We take cash and there is also an ATM inside our dispensary for your convenience.

02Is your dispensary open late?

We understand that normal operating hours are not always convenient for our consumers with busy lifestyles, which is why our dispensary is open every day from 9:00 am to 9:00 pm.

03Does Star Buds offer any discounts or ways to save on orders?

We run product specials seven days a week, and our loyalty rewards program offers even more savings. We are happy to provide our MED card holders and veterans with a discount upon request.
